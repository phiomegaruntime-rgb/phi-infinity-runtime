# PHI-INFINITY AMENDMENT 08
## Pairwise Trusted Grounding / Distinct Physical Trace Instance Semantics

THIS AMENDMENT ADDS NO NEW FUNDAMENTAL CAUSAL OPERATION.

THIS AMENDMENT DOES NOT DEFINE OR DERIVE REAL DIFFERENCE.

THIS AMENDMENT DOES NOT DEFINE SOURCE SEMANTICS.

THIS AMENDMENT DOES NOT DEFINE HOW PHYSICAL INSTANCES BECOME DISTINCT.

It defines only how an externally trusted pairwise acquisition assertion
that two already-grounded trace instances are not the same physical instance
is represented, scoped, audited, and admitted as a grounded mechanical premise.

## 1. Authority boundary

This amendment extends the trusted acquisition grounding interface of
Amendment 07 without changing Amendment 07, the Mother Mechanics, the
Fundamental Laws, or any registered mechanical inference rule.

Its execution roles are:

- PAIRWISE_TRUSTED_GROUNDING_SEMANTICS;
- EXTERNAL_EPISTEMIC_ACQUISITION_STRUCTURAL_INTERFACE;
- NON_GENERATIVE_PAIRWISE_GROUNDING_SUBSTRATE.

It is not FUNDAMENTAL_CAUSAL_MECHANICS and is not a
MECHANICAL_INFERENCE_RULE.

Installing this amendment registers no trust root, no assertion, no grounded
premise, and no mechanical conclusion.

## 2. Non-circular pairwise semantics

PHYSICAL_INSTANCE_NON_IDENTITY is not REAL_DIFFERENCE_EXISTS.

A pairwise premise means only that two exact, already-grounded acquisition
trace referents are externally accepted as two non-identical physical
acquisition-trace instances.

It does not specify or imply by constructor semantics what differs, where or
when anything differs, configuration difference, relational-position
difference, genealogy difference, state difference, payload difference,
channel difference, source difference, magnitude, direction, distance,
causal explanation, or REAL_DIFFERENCE_EXISTS.

The runtime does not prove pairwise non-identity. It records that, under an
explicitly trusted pairwise grounding contract, the two exact trace referents
are accepted as not the same physical acquisition-trace instance.

The proposition origin is GROUNDED_EXTERNAL, not DERIVED_M.

## 3. Distinct-instance proposition type

DISTINCT_REAL_ACQUISITION_TRACE_INSTANCES means:

Two exact acquisition-trace referents, each already established through
authorized trusted grounding as a mechanically instantiated acquisition
trace, are externally accepted within one exact pair scope as two
non-identical physical acquisition-trace instances.

The proposition asserts only pairwise physical-instance non-identity. It does
not independently create or duplicate either parent
REAL_ACQUISITION_TRACE_EXISTS proposition.

Its constructor policy is TRUSTED_GROUNDING_ONLY. Caller construction, raw
access construction, representation construction, and mechanical-rule
construction are not authorized by this amendment.

The proposition is witness-free and creates no difference object.

## 4. Exact parent requirements

A pairwise assertion requires exactly two valid parent
REAL_ACQUISITION_TRACE_EXISTS propositions.

Each parent must originate through authorized trusted grounding, retain its
exact grounding assertion and physical trace referent, remain historically
valid, and be currently applicable for current pairwise admission.

The pairwise assertion must preserve the exact parent proposition and receipt
identities. These are premise referents, not known-difference endpoints.

Pairwise grounding does not create either parent trace.

The same grounded trace proposition or the same grounded physical trace
referent cannot occupy both positions. Distinct(T,T) is invalid.

## 5. Symmetric semantic identity and historical ordering

Physical-instance non-identity is symmetric:

Distinct(T1,T2) has the same semantic proposition identity as
Distinct(T2,T1).

Canonical pair identity therefore uses the unordered exact parent receipt
pair. Reversing assertion order cannot fabricate a second semantic physical
relation.

Original assertion ordering, assertion identity, validation identity, and
genealogy remain immutable historical records. Multiple exact proof
genealogies may support one semantic proposition identity and are evaluated
independently for current applicability.

## 6. Prohibited constructors

No pairwise proposition may be inferred from different trace IDs, assertion
IDs, payloads, hashes, timestamps, channels, files, AccessRecords,
Amendment 04 outcomes, basis-local discrimination, genealogies, positions,
configurations, labels, statistics, probability, confidence, majority,
model judgment, or a human statement alone.

Different representation tokens do not establish physical-instance
non-identity. Representation multiplicity is not physical-instance
multiplicity.

An exact trusted pairwise assertion is mandatory.

## 7. Pairwise grounding contract and trust

The pairwise grounding contract role is
PAIRWISE_PHYSICAL_TRACE_INSTANCE_NON_IDENTITY.

It requires exact assertion identity, exact contract identity/version/hash,
an explicitly pairwise-capable trust root, exactly two parent grounded trace
receipts and trace referents, one exact pair scope, immutable provenance,
historical and current applicability, source-semantics exclusion,
real-difference exclusion, and an explicit externally accepted
physical-instance non-identity claim.

Trusting T1 exists and trusting T2 exists does not automatically authorize
pairwise non-identity. The pair assertion requires explicit pairwise trust
authorization.

The two trace parents and the pair assertion may be authorized by different
trust roots. If T1 has roots G1, T2 has roots G2, and the pair assertion has
root G3, the pair proposition ancestry is the deterministic set union
G1 union G2 union G3.

No root is ranked, substituted, inferred by majority, or installed by
contract presence. One root may authorize all required assertions only when
that authorization is explicit.

## 8. Exact pair scope

The pairwise assertion must carry and authorize one exact pair scope.

No union, intersection, least-common scope, implicit globalization, or other
scope-composition rule is inferred from parent scopes.

The exact pair scope is the maximum scope of the pairwise proposition. If no
exact pair scope is authorized, no pairwise proposition is created.

## 9. Copy, replay, and identical payload

Copying one grounded physical trace into two representations or AccessRecords
does not create a pairwise proposition.

Replaying one historically grounded trace or creating a new AccessPackage for
that replay does not create a second current physical acquisition-trace
instance.

Two independently grounded physical trace instances may have identical
payload representations and still receive a pairwise proposition when the
exact pairwise trust root and assertion exist. Payload equality neither
merges physical-instance identities nor proves their non-identity.

## 10. Source and real-difference firewall

The pairwise proposition implies no source identity, source non-identity,
source difference, source property, source event, source persistence,
calibration truth, causal relation, sensor interpretation, or domain
mechanics. It concerns only the acquisition-trace instances.

A valid pairwise premise creates no REAL_DIFFERENCE_EXISTS,
REAL_CONSEQUENCE_EXISTS, DERIVED_M proposition, difference witness, or
consequence witness.

Installing pairwise grounding is not registering the Fundamental Law of
Non-Identity. No registered mechanical inference rule consumes the pairwise
proposition under this amendment.

## 11. Generic proof-kernel support

The proof kernel may validate exact proposition type, current applicability,
explicitly permitted origin class, exact scope, exact subject receipt
requirements, genealogy integrity, and grounding ancestry.

It may not decide distinctness, difference, consequence, causation,
transformation, interaction, or source semantics.

For an authorized future mechanical-rule conclusion Q with exact proposition
premises P_i, grounding roots propagate as:

Roots(Q) = union Roots(P_i).

This is deterministic provenance-set union, not substantive mechanical
Dependency. Root-free premises produce a root-free result. Exact parent
receipt identities remain preserved and unrelated proof paths remain
separate.

Generic infrastructure alone generates no mechanical proposition.

## 12. History, revocation, and applicability

Contracts, roots, assertions, propositions, validations, and proof
genealogies are immutable historical records.

Revocation does not erase a historical pair assertion or proposition.
Revocation of a required pair root, pair assertion, or parent ancestry may
make the affected genealogy currently inapplicable. Independent alternate
genealogies may remain applicable.

Revocation creates no real-difference statement and no mechanical
consequence.

## 13. Execution frontier

After an exact pairwise grounded premise is admitted and no registered
mechanical inference rule consumes it, execution stops UNRESOLVED at:

NON_IDENTITY_RULE_REGISTRATION_FRONTIER.

This frontier means that the exact externally grounded pairwise non-identity
premise exists but no frozen registered mechanical inference rule consumes
it.

It is not M failure, REOPEN, FirstDivergence, proof of a negative fact, or
evidence that a new fundamental causal operation is required.

The single-trace frontier remains unchanged.
